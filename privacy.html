Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

OmniFit (“the App”) is a health and fitness tool. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

What stays on your device

Workout logs, nutrition entries, water and steps, profile settings, and similar day-to-day data are stored locally on your device (for example in browser or app storage). Body composition photos are stored in on-device storage (IndexedDB). We do not sell your health data.

Meal photos and AI estimates

If you use meal scan, the photo you choose is sent to a scan service so calories and macros can be estimated (currently powered by an xAI model when that service is configured). Estimates are approximate. Do not upload photos of other people without permission.

Body photos

Body photos you add for progress comparison remain on your device unless you later choose a sync or backup feature (none ships today). Delete them anytime in the App.

Health logging

Logged workouts, meals, and related metrics are for personal fitness tracking. The App is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Accounts and payments

The current build has no account system and no in-app purchases. If that changes, this policy will be updated before those features ship.
